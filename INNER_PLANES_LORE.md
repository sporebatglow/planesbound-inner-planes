## A. Traditional Inner Ring structure (prototype canon)

The atlas keeps the classic elemental wheel layout:

| Role | Planes |
|------|--------|
| **Elemental cardinals** | Fire (top), Water (bottom), Air (left), Earth (right) |
| **Energy poles** | Positive Energy, Negative Energy |
| **Transitive curtain** | Ethereal (center mist — the fog between Material / Inner shores) |
| **Para-elemental seams** | Magma (Fire ⊕ Earth), Ooze (Earth ⊕ Water), Ice (Water ⊕ Air), Smoke (Fire ⊕ Air) |

### Why this layout

- The four elementals sit on a **symmetrical ring** so opposite elements face each other (Fire↔Water, Air↔Earth).
- **Para-seams** sit on the diagonals between adjacent cardinals: they are border regions of mixed matter, not full independent cosmologies in this prototype.
- **Positive / Negative** sit as energy poles of the ring (overgrowth vs appetite), not as “alignment” heavens.
- The **Ethereal** is drawn as the central mist because, in this atlas, it is the **curtain-road** that Planesbound keepers use from the Lighthouse toward Inner shores. Travel beyond that curtain-road is not invented here.

Space-station and City of Shade imagery appears across Inner Planes art as **PLANESBOUND atlas presentation** (visual motifs). Shade skyline / street / docks art ties visually to Negative / umbra tone. Do **not** treat those images as a Forgotten Realms city chronicle.
